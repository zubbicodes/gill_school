A welcome from the family

Where it all started

Welcome to Gill Group. Over the past 55 years, our company has grown from one car and one truck — started by my late father, Tiernan Gill Senior — into Ireland's leading fleet transport education and training company.

In 2014 we were proud to receive the award for safety in transport at the Fleet Transport Awards. Today, over 40 people are associated with the company in training, and our clients include multinationals at home and abroad.

Through all of it, one thing has never changed: the customer is number 1.

— The Gill Family, Ballina
