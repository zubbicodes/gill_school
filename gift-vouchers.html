Questions

Voucher FAQs

What can a voucher be used for?

Gift cards can be put towards any lesson, EDT package or training course at Gills Driving School. Lesson vouchers are for the specific lesson shown.

How long is a voucher valid?

Validity terms to be confirmed by Gills — e.g. 12 months from the date of purchase.

Does the recipient need a learner permit?

Yes — to take a driving lesson on public roads the recipient needs a valid learner permit for that vehicle category. Theory class vouchers don't require a permit.

How do they book their lessons?

Simply call our office on 096 73030 or email info@gillgroup.ie with the voucher code.
