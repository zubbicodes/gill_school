Welcome to Gill's Driving School

From one car and one truck to Ireland's leading driver training family.

Gill Group is headquartered in Ballina, Co. Mayo — a family-run organisation in operation for 55 years, made up of five companies.

It all started with one car and one truck, driven by the late Tiernan Gill Senior. Today, over 40 people are involved in training with us, and our clients include multinationals at home and abroad.
